**Choice:** Option 1: MDOB064048AV-WI Midas OLED Screen

**Rationale:** The choice is really between the first and last options; the LCD screen is simply overkill for the project's needs. And between those two options, while the E-paper display has its appeal especially with its on-component ram, perfect size for the envisioned project, and low power consumption, the OLED screen's simpler control setup and high-contrast screen make for a better tradeoff, especially as its max power draw is lower than the E-paper's (20 mA vs 25 mA, though the E-paper's power draw is intermittent, hence the lower overall consumption).

**Choice:** Option 1: L7812CV SMTMicroelectronics

**Rationale:** The second option is out of the running since it lacks the output current to support the screen, leaving it between the first and last options. However, since the first option is slightly more expensive than the third and the difference in performance between the two is pretty negligible (Quiescent current is essentially a non-factor for our purposes), the third is the best choice.
