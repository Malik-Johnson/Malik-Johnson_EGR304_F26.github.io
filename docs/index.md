## Introduction

This datasheet provides an account of the design process and purpose of the elements of the HeatSmith device designed by Malik Johnson.

### Project Summary
The HeatSmith device is a product designed to provide users with an up-to-date accurate reading of the temperature and humidity.


### My Contribution
This portion of the HeatSmith device is the data processing/UX portion. The design goal here is to balance usability with information delivery and do so at a reasonable cost in both dollars and power. More detail on the rest of the HeatSmith device and the relation between the part described in this datasheet to the wider device found in the [team report.](https://egr304-2026-f-106.github.io/EGR304-2026-F-2026.github.io/)
To review the details listed of the material used to construct the subsection, you can review it in the ["BOM"](https://malik-johnson.github.io/Malik-Johnson_EGR304_F26.github.io/03-BOM/BOM/) section of the datasheet.
